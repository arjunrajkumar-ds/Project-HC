Food logging & day-fill

wireframes · home · 2026-10-18

The home-page parallel to the stage bar: make logging a food feel great, and give the day a quiet filling-up narrative. One plate ring (calories + protein toward goal) sits above the meal columns; adding an entry lands it in its meal, rolls the counters, slides the macro fills, lights the meal's coverage dot, and — only when a goal line is crossed — plays a one-shot flourish. Light completion by design: no streaks, no scores. Reuses the existing macroBarUpdate() / flAnimateBarGrowth() / bar-popup language from _macro_bar.html and home.html. Pandora tokens only; meal tints are the one hardcoded family (parallel to MG_COLORS). Everything works at 375px; all motion is off under prefers-reduced-motion.

01

Logging a food — the entry lands

Tap a meal's + add, type (or tap a recent chip), submit. The row drops into the meal with a spring, the meal total ticks up, the plate ring + macro bars slide to their new value, and a +kcal badge floats off the ring. A food with no macros yet routes to the define sheet (03) instead of animating.

Simulate
  1. Land. New row animates entryland .5s — drops in, settles, accent wash fades.
  2. Roll. Meal total + ring % count up; macro fills slide (.55s) with a lighter grow-overlay on top.
  3. Badge. +129 floats off the ring for 1s.
  4. Recents. Chips fill the name only — never a quantity (matches flSetName).

02

Day-fill — the plate ring reaching goal

The ring is calories (outer arc, accent) with protein as the inner readout; the two macro bars sit beside it, and a row of four coverage dots shows which meals have anything logged. Crossing a goal line fills the arc, pulses a ringflood halo once, and shows a goal tag. Over-goal turns the arc + bar --red and the readout shows the overage — never a failure state, just a colour.

Simulate
  1. Arc. stroke-dashoffset eases .6s to the new %; cap rounded.
  2. Reach. At ≥100% the ring gets hit (accent number) + one ringflood pulse + a goal tag.
  3. Over. Arc + fill flip to --red; readout reads +120 kcal.
  4. Dots. Each covered meal lights in its tint; newly-covered one pops (dotpop).
